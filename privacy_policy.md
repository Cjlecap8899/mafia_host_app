Privacy Policy

Mafia Host Assistant does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The application works completely offline and does not require an internet connection. 
No analytics services, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking tools are used.

All game-related data is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

The app does not request access to personal information such as contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the project repository.
